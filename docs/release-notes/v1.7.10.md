# Data Center AddOn 1.7.10

Part 11 of the new customer model: **competitors**. Fictional hosting companies take part of every market. Good times let them grow, the crash sends some into bankruptcy, and new ones are founded.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Competitors
- Every world gets **5 competitors** on its first day, with invented names (Bitmühle, Datenkai, Quillnet, …). Each sells about 60 % of the products and holds a share of those markets (around 8 %).
- **Every game day:**
  - Each competitor's share wanders a little: up in good times, down in bad ones, and always back towards its usual size (between 2 % and 25 %).
  - **Bankruptcy:** about 14 % a year in normal times. During the dotcom crash (market mood 0.63) it rises to about 50 % a year. Weak competitors go under more easily.
  - **New competitors** are founded about once every 18 months in good times, none in a crash, at most 7 at once.
- **Effect:** a product's competition is the summed share of the competitors that sell it. Your potential is scaled by **(1 − competition) / 0.75**: at the usual 25 % nothing changes, at 10 % your potential is 20 % higher, at 40 % it is 20 % lower.
- All accounts share the same competitors.
- Bankruptcies and new competitors are announced in chat: "[Market] Datenkai is bankrupt. Its Shared Web, VPS customers (9 % of the market) are looking for a new host."
- Competitors are moved on day by day, also after a time skip (at most 60 days); only today's changes are announced.

## Market tab
- Under the table: the competitors and their shares. Hover to see which products each one sells.
- The tooltip of **Reachable** shows the competition of the product and its factor.
