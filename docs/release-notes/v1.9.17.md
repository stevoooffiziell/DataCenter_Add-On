# Data Center AddOn 1.9.17

**New pre-built servers that change with the date.** The add-on replaces Data Center's pre-built servers with its own line: seven kinds of server, put together anew every quarter from 1998 to 2026 from the parts on sale at that date, including two efficient servers for the early game.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · JEI 19 (for the recipe pages)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## The servers
| Server | Built for | Markup |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter Server** (early game) | the most web hosting customers per dollar, at most twice the price of the cheapest complete server | 10 % |
| **Eco Server** (early game) | the most customers per watt, at most three times the price of the cheapest | 15 % |
| **Web Hosting Server** | many customers in one server, usually dual CPU, for full racks | 25 % |
| **VPS Host** | the most VPS per dollar | 25 % |
| **Game Server** | the most game slots per dollar | 25 % |
| **Dedicated Server** | the most rent per dollar (data-centre CPUs earn three times as much), with GPUs where they pay | 30 % |
| **Storage Server** | every drive bay filled with the drives that give the most GB per dollar | 25 % |

- Each quarter only uses parts released in the last three years. CPU and board are chosen with the hosting rack's own capacity formula for that date, RAM and disks to match the compute.
- When the best build of a server stays the same, it simply stays on sale.
- The add-on's real parts count too; parts hidden with `hideOriginalParts` are not used.
- Price: the chassis and the parts at today's shop prices, plus the markup of the server.
- Every server has a short description in the shop.

## Removed
- Data Center's own pre-built servers: budget, performance and high-end, the compute and hosting builds, the mining servers, and the complete servers of 1998 to 2008 (new and used).
