# Data Center AddOn 1.9.7

Part 8 of the add-on's industry: **using your own components.** Cables and the first switches can be crafted from what your factory makes, and delivery orders now ask for components too.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## New recipes
| Result | Recipe |
|---|---|
| **16 × CAT3 cable** | 2 copper wire + plastic parts (shapeless). The old recipe (2 copper ingots + string) gives 8. |
| **12 × OM3 fibre cable** | 6 glass panes around 2 plastic parts and silicon (like the old recipe with plastic parts instead of string and silicon instead of quartz; it gives 8) |
| **3Com SuperStack II hub** | 3 plastic parts, 2 connectors, a circuit board, 2 capacitors, a microchip |
| **Cisco Catalyst 2924-XL** | 2 plastic parts, 4 connectors, 2 circuit boards, a microchip, a capacitor |

- The other cables come from these by the existing upgrade recipes (CAT3 → CAT5 → … and OM3 → OM4 → OS2).
- Only the two switches that are on sale from the start of every world (1993 and 1997) can be crafted. The newer ones stay in the Shop from their release date.
- **Sell-back:** crafted cables and switches carry the value of the components they were made of. Data Center's Shop buys them back for at most 80 % of that, not of the Shop price, so crafting is no way around the Sales Terminal's market.

## Delivery orders with components
- About one goal in ten now asks for factory components: circuit boards, capacitors, connectors, microchips, copper wire or plastic parts.
- The amounts follow the price: 4–12 microchips, 8–24 circuit boards or connectors, 16–48 of the cheaper ones (more with a higher customer class).
- They pay **1.6 times** what the Sales Terminal pays at full demand, before the class multiplier, and they are not touched by its market saturation.
- Switch deliveries now pay off for a factory too: a crafted hub fulfils a hub order worth 1.25 × its Shop price.
