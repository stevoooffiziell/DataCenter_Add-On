# Data Center AddOn 1.8.10

Part 11 of the new reputation model: **advertising.** Money for renown, with the campaigns of the era.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Campaigns

| Campaign | Era | Cost | Renown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Banner | until 2005 | $500 | +4 |
| Magazine ad | until 2012 | $1,500 | +10 |
| Search ads | from 2001 | $800 | +6 |
| Social media | from 2008 | $1,000 | +8 |
| Video | from 2014 | $2,500 | +18 |
| Trade fair | always | $5,000 | +35 |

- Each campaign can be booked **once every 7 game days**.
- The money is taken from your wallet at once; the renown comes at once.
- Advertising is a shortcut to the renown a class needs, not a way around the Ruf: a class still needs its quality (1.8.4).

## Reputation tab
At the bottom a row **Advertising** with a button per campaign of the era: name and price, or the days until it can be booked again. Click twice to book. Hover for cost and renown.
