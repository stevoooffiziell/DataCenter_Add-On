# Data Center AddOn 1.9.5

Part 6 of the add-on's industry: the **chip production line**, a multiblock factory. It turns silicon wafers into **microchips**, the most valuable component of the chain. The bigger and better equipped the line, the faster it runs.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## The blocks
All three are in the Shop (Infrastructure).

| Block | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Chip Fab Controller** | $40,000 | The heart of the line: inventory, power, screen. Cyan front. |
| **Cleanroom Wall** | $200 | White panels; the cleanroom around the line |
| **Lithography Unit** | $15,000 | Each one makes the line one step faster; glows violet |

## Building the line
- The line is the controller and **every cleanroom wall and lithography unit that touches it or each other** (face to face, up to 96 blocks). The shape is up to you: a wall, a room, a corridor.
- It needs at least **8 cleanroom walls** and **1 lithography unit**.
- **Speed:** each lithography unit adds one step (×1, ×2 … up to ×8), but every unit needs **4 cleanroom walls**. Units without enough walls stand idle.
  - Example: 12 walls and 3 units = ×3. 12 walls and 4 units = still ×3.
- The line is checked every two seconds; the screen shows walls, units and the speed, or what is missing.

## Microchips

| Inputs | Output | Time at ×1 | Power at ×1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silicon wafer + 2 gold contacts + 2 redstone | 4 × **Microchips** | 20 s | 80 FE/t |

- Every speed step shortens the time and draws that much more power per tick: ×4 makes 4 chips in 5 s at 320 FE/t.
- The controller has **three input** and two output slots and a buffer of **200,000 FE** (up to 8,000 FE per tick in).
- Automation as with the other machines: hoppers and pipes fill the inputs (one kind per slot) and empty the outputs, from any side of the controller. Walls and units have no inventory.

## The full chain to a microchip
Purchase Terminal (raw gold, quartz, coal, redstone) → Smelter (gold ingot, silicon) → Rolling Mill (gold contacts) + Press (wafers) → **Chip production line**.
