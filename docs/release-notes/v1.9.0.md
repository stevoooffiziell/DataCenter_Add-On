# Data Center AddOn 1.9.0

The start of the add-on's **industry**: build factories, buy raw materials, process them into components and sell them. The 1.9.x releases add one piece each. First piece: the **Purchase Terminal**, which buys raw materials faster than any mining simulation or synthesizer can make them.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Purchase Terminal
A new block in the Shop (Infrastructure, $4,000). It belongs to the company (wallet account) of the player who places it; only that company can use it.

### Raw materials

| Material | 1999 | Price curve |
|---|---|---|
| Raw copper | $0.80 | real copper market (×4 by 2006, ×5.6 in 2011, ×6 in 2025) |
| Raw iron | $0.30 | inflation (2.5 % a year) |
| Raw gold | $9.00 | real gold market ($280 an ounce in 1999 → $1,600 in 2012 → $2,900 in 2025) |
| Quartz | $1.20 | inflation |
| Sand | $0.05 | inflation |
| Redstone | $1.50 | inflation |
| Coal | $0.10 | inflation |
| **Plastic pellets** (new item) | $0.30 | follows crude oil |

### Budget and wallet
- **Budget:** pay money in from your wallet (+$100, +$1K). Purchases are paid from it. **Pay out** returns it to the wallet. Breaking the terminal pays the budget back and drops its stock.
- **Wallet access** (button): when it is on and the budget is not enough, purchases are paid from the wallet. Off by default, so automatic purchases can never empty your wallet.

### Buying
- Per material: **+1** and **+stack** buy at once, as far as the money and the terminal's space allow.
- **Auto-reorder:** per material a target stock (steps of 16, up to 576). Every two seconds the terminal buys what is missing.
- The goods go into the terminal's **stock** (18 slots). You can take them out by hand, or let **hoppers, pipes and other automation pull them out** from any side. Nothing can be put in.
