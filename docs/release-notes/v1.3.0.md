# Data Center AddOn 1.3.0

Real network cables and switches, from CAT3 to single-mode fibre, and a Hosting Manager that shows and controls all hosting racks on its network.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
Install on the **server and every client**.

## Network cables
- **9 real cables**, each with its own colour, its real release date and its real link speed:

| Cable | Speed | Compute/t | Reach | From |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CAT3 | 10 Mbit/s | 2,500 | 100 | 1991 |
| CAT5 | 100 Mbit/s | 25,000 | 100 | 1995 |
| CAT5e | 1 Gbit/s | 250,000 | 100 | 1999 |
| CAT6 | 10 Gbit/s | 2.5 M | 55 | 2002 |
| CAT6a | 10 Gbit/s | 2.5 M | 100 | 2008 |
| CAT8 | 40 Gbit/s | 10 M | 30 | 2016 |
| OM3 fibre (multimode) | 10 Gbit/s | 2.5 M | 300 | 2002 |
| OM4 fibre (multimode) | 100 Gbit/s | 25 M | 150 | 2009 |
| OS2 fibre (single-mode) | 400 Gbit/s | 100 M | 10,000 | 2017 |

- Capacity follows the real speed: 1 Gbit/s = 250,000 compute per tick, the same as Data Center's twisted-pair stage, so every step up is a real step.
- **Reach:** a cable run longer than its cable's real maximum length falls back to a tenth of its speed, like a link that drops to the next slower standard. Switches start a new run.
- They join Data Center's compute network and connect to racks, machines and Data Center's own cables.
- **Shop** from their release date (Infrastructure), or **craft** them: CAT3 from copper and string, OM3 from glass panes, string and quartz. Upgrade like Mekanism: 8 cables around one material give 8 cables of the next tier (redstone, iron, gold, quartz, diamond; amethyst and ender pearl for fibre).

## Network switches
- **8 real switches:** 3Com SuperStack II Hub, Cisco Catalyst 2924-XL, 2950 and 3750G, Cisco Nexus 5010, Arista 7050S-64 and 7060CX-32S, NVIDIA Spectrum SN4700. Each with its real switching capacity and power draw.
- **Without a switch** all cables form one run and the weakest cable limits the network, as before.
- **With switches** every cable run counts on its own: each run carries its own traffic, and the switch's backplane limits the total. A star (one run per rack) now beats a chain.
- **Switches need power** (FE, their real wattage). A switch without power cuts its network.

## Hosting Manager
- A management terminal: connect it to the network of your hosting racks with any cable.
- **Overview:** customers, income per day and per month, number of racks, reputation and customer class, the network's load and capacity with warnings (switch without power, cable run too long), and a table per product.
- **Customers:** every customer segment (online shops, game servers, streaming, ...) with its number of customers, share and income.
- **Racks:** every rack with product, customers and capacity, share, income and status. Select one to change its **product** and **fill** right from the manager, just like in the rack's own menu.
- Updates every second. Racks of other players in the same network are counted but not listed.
- Shop (Infrastructure, $2,500, from world start) or craft it.

## Settings
New in `config/data_center_addon-common.toml`, section `network`:
- `hideOriginalNetwork` (default `false`): hides Data Center's own cables and switches. Its switch leaves the Shop and its cable recipes are disabled; placed ones keep working. The superconducting cable stays and is then crafted with OS2 fibre instead of Data Center's fibre.
- `switchesNeedPower` (default `true`)
- `cableReachLimits` (default `true`)

## Languages
English and German.

## Known limitations
- Data Center's network analyzer and the Jade overlay show the correct capacity, but when one of this add-on's cables or switches limits the network they do not name the limiting cable. The Hosting Manager shows the add-on's cable warnings.
- Customer segments: Data Center keeps one customer book per player and product. The manager shows the part of it that sits on the racks of its network; the income per segment is split by what each segment pays, not tracked separately by Data Center.
- CAT6 runs at 10 Gbit/s from 2002, although 10GBASE-T only came in 2006. Switch prices are approximate list prices.
- The new cables and switches rely on Data Center 2.1.3's network code. If a later Data Center version changes it, the cables keep connecting but stop limiting the network.
