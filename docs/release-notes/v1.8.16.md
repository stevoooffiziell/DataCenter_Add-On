# Data Center AddOn 1.8.16

Part 17 of the new reputation model: **the reputation explained in one line.** The Hosting Manager says what your Ruf and renown are made of.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Breakdown
- **Overview:** under the renown and network bars a line like
  "Ruf 94 % (uptime 99.2 %, 1 crashes, 0.0 % overload) · Renown 312 (+40 Orders finished, +12 Customer reviews …)".
  - The Ruf part covers the last 30 days.
  - The renown part lists what changed your renown **in the last 30 game days**, largest first. Hover for all sources.
- **Reputation tab:** hover over the top line for the same breakdown. The list on the right still shows the totals since 1.8.3.
- The add-on now keeps the renown of every source per day for 30 days.
