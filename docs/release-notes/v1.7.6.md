# Data Center AddOn 1.7.6

Part 7 of the new customer model: a **waiting list**. New customers who find no free place wait a few days instead of being lost at once, and the Hosting Manager tells you to build capacity.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Waiting list
For shared hosting, VPS and colocation, per product:
- New customers who find no free place go on the waiting list.
- They wait **7 in-game days** on average; every day about one in seven gives up.
- When places become free (a new rack, a higher fill, cancellations), the waiting customers get them first.
- Waiting customers count against your potential: the gap that brings new customers is potential − customers − waiting. The list therefore never grows beyond what you could reach.
- Before, customers without a free place were simply lost.

## Hosting Manager
- **Overview:** a warning line "12 customers are waiting for a free place: build capacity."
- **Market tab:** the waiting customers appear in amber next to the customers of their product (e.g. "310 +12"); hover for details.

## Config
New option in the `market` section:

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `waitDays` | 7.0 | In-game days new customers without a free place wait on average (0 = off) |
