# Data Center AddOn 1.6.0

Getting started is no longer a long wait. A young hosting company now gets a start bonus, a founding grant, an interest-free founding loan, setup fees from every new customer, milestone rewards and small jobs that pay at once. Everything lives in a new **Startup** tab in the Hosting Manager.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**.

## Startup tab
A fifth tab in the Hosting Manager. It shows:
- **Left:** the start bonus, the founding grant, the setup fees and the founding loan.
- **Right:** all milestones, with their rewards.
- **Bottom:** the open service tickets.

The tab also works when the manager is not plugged into a network.

## Founding phase
- **Start bonus:** every hosting rack earns an extra **+200 %** on top of its income. The bonus starts with your first rack income and fades out over **120 in-game days**. In `REVENUE` mode it pays the full bonus instead until your racks have earned $50,000, then it ends. You get a message when it starts and when it ends.
- **Founding grant:** **$2,000**, paid once with your first rack income.
- **Founding loan:** **$10,000**, interest-free, taken once with two clicks in the Startup tab.
  - The first **30 days** need no repayment. After that the loan is paid back in **60 daily instalments**.
  - If the wallet is short, the missing part is due with the next instalments.
  - "Repay" pays back as much as the wallet allows.
- **Setup fees:** every **new customer** pays a one-time fee of one month of its product's list price, times your customer class multiplier. This counts for shared hosting, VPS and colocation.
  - Example: a shared web customer in 1999 pays $25, a colocation customer $125.
- **Established companies:** an account that already has 150 reputation the first time the add-on sees it gets no start bonus and no grant. Existing worlds keep their balance.

## Service tickets
- Now and then a client asks you to look at one of your running racks: a restart, a backup, the logs, the cabling or a noisy fan.
- Each ticket shows the rack's position and pays **$30–80**, more with a higher customer class.
- **Right-click the rack** before the deadline (today or tomorrow) and you are paid at once.
- You can have up to 2 tickets open. An expired ticket costs nothing.

## Contracts
- **Emergency orders ⚡:** about one offer in seven. They last 2–4 days, pay **2.5×** and can only be accepted on the day they appear. They are marked in amber.
- **Mini orders:** while your customer class is *Hobbyists*, many offers ask for only **1–5 places** for 3–6 days. There is no penalty and no reputation loss if they fail. They are marked "Mini".

## Delivery orders
Delivery orders were in the 1.5.3 preview. They are now part of the release, with two additions:
- **For a young company:** while the founding phase runs, orders are simpler. They have one goal (cables of the time, or 1–3 memory modules, fans, drives or defective parts) and 2 more days to deliver. You can have up to **4 offers**, and **2 new ones** arrive every day.
- **Scrap:** clients now also buy **defective parts**: any defective CPU, graphics card, memory, drive, power supply or fan, at $15 each before the class multiplier. Data Center's Shop already buys back working parts.

## Config
New section `startup` in `data_center_addon-common.toml`:

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `startBonus` | 2.0 | Extra share of rack income paid on top (0 = off) |
| `startBonusMode` | TIME | `TIME`: fades out over the days; `REVENUE`: full bonus until the revenue is reached |
| `startBonusDays` | 120 | In-game days the TIME bonus fades out over |
| `startBonusRevenueUsd` | 50000 | Revenue until which the REVENUE bonus runs |
| `foundingGrantUsd` | 2000 | Founding grant (0 = off) |
| `foundingLoanUsd` | 10000 | Founding loan (0 = off) |
| `foundingLoanGraceDays` | 30 | Days before the first instalment |
| `foundingLoanRepayDays` | 60 | Number of daily instalments |
| `veteranReputation` | 150 | Reputation from which an account counts as established |
| `setupFeeMonths` | 1.0 | Setup fee in months of list price (0 = off) |
| `milestoneRewards` | 1.0 | Multiplier for milestone rewards (0 = off) |
| `serviceTickets` | true | Service tickets on/off |

## Technical
The start bonus reads each rack's income through a small read-only hook into Data Center's hosting rack. If a Data Center update changes that method, the start bonus and the service tickets stop silently. The game does not crash.
