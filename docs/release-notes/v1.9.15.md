# Data Center AddOn 1.9.15

**Fix: Data Center SkyBlock worlds with SkyWorlds installed.** Joining a new "Data Center SkyBlock" world disconnected you right after the terrain loaded ("Invalid player data") when the SkyWorlds mod was installed.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · JEI 19 (for the recipe pages)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Fix
- The world type's overworld had a single layer of air. Minecraft replaces such layers internally with an empty entry when the biome is not the vanilla void, and SkyWorlds' check for void worlds crashed on it while placing the player.
- The overworld now has no layers at all. SkyWorlds leaves the world alone, since the add-on's island already stands at the spawn.
- Worlds created with 1.9.14 keep the old layer: create a new world.
