# Data Center AddOn 1.5.3-preview

**Preview build on top of 1.5.2, not a release.** It adds delivery orders to the Hosting Manager so you can try them out. Figures and layout may still change. Use a test world or a backup.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests · Jade 15
Install on the **server and every client**.

## Delivery orders
The **Contracts** tab of the Hosting Manager now has two views:
- **Reservations:** the hosting contracts from 1.5.0 (keep places free), unchanged.
- **Deliveries** (new, opens first): clients want goods delivered before a deadline.

### What clients ask for
Each order has one or two goals:
- **Servers:** an assembled server (mainboard and at least one CPU). From the third customer class on, also **GPU servers** (with at least one graphics card).
- **Parts of a kind:** CPUs, graphics cards, memory, drives, power supplies or fans. Any model counts, as long as it is not defective.
- **This add-on's cables and switches:** only those that exist at the current in-game date. Newer models are asked for more often.

The amounts and the pay grow with the customer class your reputation has unlocked.

### How it works
1. **Offers:** up to 3 at a time. A new one arrives every in-game day, and each stays open for 3 days.
2. **Accept:** the deadline starts. It is 4 to 16 days, depending on the client and the number of goals. Up to 3 deliveries can run at once.
3. **Deliver:** the "Deliver" button takes matching items from **your inventory**. Partial deliveries count. The detail panel shows how many you have delivered so far and, in brackets, how many matching items you are carrying.
4. **Done:** when every goal is complete, the client pays and your reputation rises.

A missed deadline or a cancelled order (two clicks) costs reputation. Items you have already delivered stay with the client and are not paid for. There is no money penalty.

### Layout
The left side lists running deliveries and offers with client, progress, days left and pay. Click a row to see its details on the right: the goods with item icons, progress, pay, the reputation at stake, and the buttons. Hover over an item icon to see what exactly counts.

### Config
New entry `deliveries` (on/off) under `hosting` in `data_center_addon-common.toml`. The pay follows `contractPayMultiplier`.

## Not in this preview
- **Bountiful:** no data pack yet. Bountiful for 1.21.1 exists only as a beta (8.0.0-beta.2).
- **Item slots** in the manager: delivering works with a button straight from your inventory, not by dragging items into slots as in the mockup.
- **Quest** and **Jade** info for deliveries.
- **Balancing:** pay per unit is fixed per kind of goal (e.g. $900 per server, $80 per memory module, cables at 2.5× their price), multiplied by the customer class.
