# Data Center AddOn 1.6.6

The Racks tab of the Hosting Manager now counts contract customers too.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**.

## Contract customers in the Racks tab
Contracts reserve places of a product, not of one rack. The Racks tab now spreads them over the racks of that product, in proportion to the places each rack offers. Racks that offer no places share them evenly.
- **Customers:** a rack's share of the contract places is added to its customers, as one sum. For example, 2 customers plus 3 contract places show as "5 / 13".
- **Share:** the shares include the contract places.
- **$ / game day:** a rack's share of the contracts' daily pay is added to its income.
- **Tooltip:** hover over a rack to see how the figure is made up: its own customers, the contract places and what those pay.

The Overview keeps showing contract places separately in blue ("2 + 3"). Contracts of a product with no rack on the manager's network are not counted in the Racks tab.
