# Data Center AddOn 1.7.5

Part 6 of the new customer model: **cancellations follow the quality of your service**. Good service keeps customers for years; servers that fail, run overloaded or get throttled drive them away.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Cancellations by quality
For shared hosting, VPS and colocation, per product:
- **Downtime:** share of the customers whose server did not run (no power, crashed, missing parts).
- **Overload:** how far the servers run over their real capacity (the same figure Data Center turns into complaints).
- **Throttling:** share of the time the racks ran throttled by heat, an attack or a network too slow for their customers.
- Each is averaged over about **3 game days**. Together they give the cancellations per month: **1 % with good service**, up to **8 % with bad service**. Data Center's fixed rate was about 3 %.
  - Badness = downtime + 0.6 × overload + 0.4 × throttling (at most 1).
- Segments and events still shift the rate as in Data Center (dotcom customers leave faster after the crash, for example).
- Until a product has been measured, Data Center's usual rate applies.
- Lower cancellations also raise the level a product settles at: with good service it ends close to its potential.

## Market tab
- New column **Cancel / mo.**: green up to 2.5 %, amber up to 5 %, red above.
- Hover over it to see downtime, overload and throttling.

## Config
New options in the `market` section:

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `churnGood` | 0.01 | Cancellations per game month with perfect service |
| `churnBad` | 0.08 | Cancellations per game month with bad service |

## Technical
The add-on reads each rack's service where the rack reports it to Data Center's reputation. If a Data Center update changes that call, the quality is no longer measured and Data Center's usual rate applies. The game does not crash.
