# Data Center AddOn 1.9.12

**Classes need their room.** A customer class can now only be unlocked while a rack of yours is serving in a room with the certification the class needs. Without a Tier I room you stay at **Start-ups**, however good your Ruf and renown are.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · JEI 19 (for the recipe pages)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Room requirement
- Mid-Market and E-Commerce need a **Tier I** room, Enterprise and Corporations **Tier II**, Finance to Government **Tier III**, Telecom to Critical Infrastructure **Tier IV**.
- A room only counts while at least one of your racks in it is serving customers.
- A class you already hold is **not lost** when the room goes; you just cannot climb higher until you have one again.
- **Reputation tab:** the room of the next class is marked ✔ or ✘, like Ruf and renown.
- With the reputation model off, nothing changes.
