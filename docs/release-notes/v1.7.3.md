# Data Center AddOn 1.7.3

Part 4 of the new customer model: **word of mouth**. Happy customers bring more customers, so a product that already has a customer base grows faster.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Word of mouth
- Every customer brings **0.15 new customers per game month**, as long as the reachable potential is not used up.
- The closer your customers are to the potential, the fewer come this way: **× (1 − customers / potential)**. This is logistic growth: slow with few customers, fastest at half the potential, zero when it is full.
- It comes on top of the customers that close the gap (1.7.2). New customers still only take free places.
- Example: 300 of 600 reachable customers bring 300 × 0.15 × 0.5 = **22 extra customers a month**.
- Because word of mouth adds to the inflow, a product now levels off a little closer to its potential.

## Market tab
- New column **New / month**: new customers per game month of every product.
- Hover over it to see how many close the gap and how many come by word of mouth.

## Config
New option in the `market` section:

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `wordOfMouth` | 0.15 | New customers per game month every customer brings (0 = off) |
