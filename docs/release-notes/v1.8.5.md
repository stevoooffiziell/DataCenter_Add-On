# Data Center AddOn 1.8.5

Part 6 of the new reputation model: **orders are the main source of renown.** Contracts and delivery orders now give three times as much reputation.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Orders give more renown
With the reputation model on, every new offer carries **3×** the reputation:

| Order | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Mini contract | +1 | +3 |
| Contract, Hobbyists → Finance | +3 → +19 | +9 → +57 |
| Emergency contract | +2 on top | +6 on top |
| Delivery, Hobbyists → Finance | +2 → +14 | +6 → +42 |

- **The risk grows with it:** a failed or cancelled contract still costs twice its reputation, a failed delivery its reputation, now of the higher value. Mini contracts still cost nothing.
- The Contracts tab shows the new values.
- Offers and orders that were already there keep their old values.
- Example: the renown for Small Business (100) is about 10 finished hobbyist contracts or 7 deliveries; for Mid-Market (200) a few more at the higher class.

## Config
New option in the `reputation` section:

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `orderRenown` | 3.0 | Multiplier for the reputation of contracts and delivery orders (gain and loss) |
