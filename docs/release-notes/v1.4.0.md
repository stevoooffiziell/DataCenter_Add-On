# Data Center AddOn 1.4.0

A guide and a release calendar for FTB Quests: everything the add-on does, step by step, and what comes out when.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar)
Install on the **server and every client**.

## Guide (chapter group "Data Center AddOn")
Four chapters, 22 quests, in English and German:
- **Getting Started:** what the add-on brings, real parts in the Shop, tiers from Common to Legendary, the date & prices overlay, the settings.
- **Real Parts:** assembling a server and reading its component list, the right mainboard (socket and memory generation), hiding Data Center's own parts, the storage boost for hosting.
- **Network: Cables & Switches:** your first data cable, speed and reach, a real switch and why a star beats a chain, switch power, fibre, upgrading cables, Data Center's own cables.
- **Hosting Manager:** hosting racks, building the manager, the Overview, Customers and Racks tabs, remote control, the Powered Server Room Panel.

Quests that ask you to get something (a server, a cable, a switch, the Hosting Manager, ...) pay a small reward in Data Center payment vouchers; reading quests are ticked off.

## Release Calendar (own chapter group)
- One chapter per decade: **1990s, 2000s, 2010s, 2020s**.
- **One quest for every year with releases** (32 years from 1991 to 2025). It lists every release of that year by category (CPUs, graphics cards, accelerators, mining cards, mainboards, memory, storage, power supplies, fans, cables and switches) with its date and launch price.
- **59 milestones** hang below their year, from the Celeron 300A and the first Athlon to the H100, the B200 and the RTX 5090, each with a short note on why it mattered. Own the part to complete its milestone.
- The calendar is generated from the add-on's own data, so it always matches the Shop.

## How it gets into your quest book
- When a world starts, the add-on adds its chapters to the FTB Quests quest book of the instance: two chapter groups after your own, and their texts in the lang files. **Nothing else in the quest book is touched.**
- The chapters are only rewritten when a new version of the add-on brings new ones. A chapter you deleted stays deleted until then.
- `installQuests` (new section `quests` in `config/data_center_addon-common.toml`, default `true`) turns it off, e.g. to keep your own edits of these chapters.

## Languages
English and German.

## Known limitations
- The calendar shows all years, also those before or long after the world's current date; it does not follow the in-game date.
- Updating the add-on overwrites changes made to its own chapters. Turn off `installQuests` to keep them.
