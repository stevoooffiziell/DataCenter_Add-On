# Data Center AddOn 1.6.1

**Prepayment:** part of your new customers now book an annual plan and pay several months in advance. The money is in your wallet at once, right when a young company needs it most.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**.

## How it works
- **Who prepays:** **30 %** of the new customers of shared hosting, VPS and colocation book an annual plan.
- **What you get now:** each of these customers pays **3 to 12 months** of its product in advance, with a **5 % discount**. The money arrives as soon as a whole customer has come together.
- **How it is settled:** Data Center keeps paying for these customers every month as before. The add-on settles the prepaid months against that income: each in-game day it takes one day's worth of the plan from your wallet.
  - Over the whole plan you receive the same money as without a plan, minus the discount, but months earlier.
- **Early leavers:** customers who leave before their plan ends get the rest back. The refund is also taken from your wallet.
- **Short wallet:** if the wallet cannot cover a day, the amount stays open and is taken later. Your balance never goes below zero.

## Hosting Manager
The **Startup** tab has a new line under the setup fees:
- It shows **Prepayment: $X received · $Y still to settle**.
- The **On/Off** button switches annual plans for your company. Turned off, new customers pay monthly only. Plans already running keep running.
- Hover over the line to see the share, the discount and the number of customers on a running plan.

The *Founding your company* quest mentions prepayment.

## Config
Two new options in the `startup` section of `data_center_addon-common.toml`:

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `prepayShare` | 0.3 | Share of new customers that prepay (0 = off) |
| `prepayDiscount` | 0.05 | Discount for prepaying customers |
