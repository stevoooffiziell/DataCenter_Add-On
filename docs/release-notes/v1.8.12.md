# Data Center AddOn 1.8.12

Part 13 of the new reputation model: **forgetting.** Renown fades slowly when nothing new happens.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Forgetting
- Any renown gained (an order, new customers, a good review, a campaign, an SLA month, a milestone …) counts as something new.
- After **30 game days** without anything new, renown starts to fade: **1 % per month**, a little every day.
- The next gain stops it at once.
- Example: 400 renown and 60 quiet days lose about 4 points.

## Reputation tab
- From 20 quiet days a line warns "Nothing new for 24 days", from 30 days "Being forgotten (35 quiet days) −1 %/mo.".
- New source **Forgotten** in the renown list.
