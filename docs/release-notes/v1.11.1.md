# Data Center AddOn 1.11.1

**The Room Fan is back in the shop.** Data Center took it out of the shop, and it has no recipe; the add-on sells it again, like the AC units.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · JEI 19 (for the recipe pages) · Mekanism Generators 10.7 (for the Wind Generator upgrades) · Flux Networks 8 (for the Flux tab)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Shop
- **Room Fan** for $80 under *Infrastructure*: moves air from one room to another (40 FE/t, 500 CFM).
- The AC Indoor Unit does not need a Room Fan: it cools its sealed room on its own, as long as an AC Outdoor Unit with power is connected through refrigerant pipes.

## Config
- `roomFan` (default `true`) in `[shop]`: sell the Room Fan.

## Languages
English and German.
