# Data Center AddOn 1.9.20

**Speed Upgrades for Mekanism's Wind Generator.** With Mekanism Generators installed, the Wind Generator takes up to 4 Speed Upgrades in its upgrade window, like the Metallurgic Infuser and Mekanism's other machines.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · JEI 19 (for the recipe pages) · Mekanism Generators 10.7 (for the Wind Generator upgrades)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Wind Generator
- Takes Speed Upgrades besides its Muffling Upgrades, at most 4 (Mekanism's machines take 8). The upgrade window shows the limit.
- Each upgrade raises the generation like a machine's speed: `× maxUpgradeMultiplier^(upgrades / 8)`. With Mekanism's default multiplier of 10, that is ×3.16 with 4 upgrades.
- The most the generator hands on per tick grows by the same factor, so the extra energy can leave.
- Without Mekanism Generators nothing changes.

## Config
New section `[mekanism]` in `data_center_addon-common.toml`:
- `windSpeedUpgrades` (0–8, default `4`): how many Speed Upgrades a Wind Generator takes. 0 = none, as in Mekanism. Needs a restart.
