# Data Center AddOn 1.9.18

**Less pressure from new customers, and more to buy in the shop.** New clients and contract offers only come while you have free places, the AC Indoor and Outdoor Units can be bought again, and the shop sells every Mekanism transport tier, bigger energy cubes and the induction matrix.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · JEI 19 (for the recipe pages)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Customers only when you have room
- **New customers:** if a product has no places on offer, or all of them are taken, no new orders come in and the waiting list stops growing. Once a place is free again, customers come back.
- **Contract offers:** clients only ask for products with free places, and never for more places than are free. Places already promised to running contracts do not count as free. With no free places at all, no new offer comes that day.
- **Accepting:** an offer can only be accepted while its places are really free. Otherwise you get a message instead of a contract that starts collecting warnings right away.

## Shop
- **AC Indoor Unit and AC Outdoor Unit** are sold again (Data Center took them out of the shop, and they have no recipe).
- **Mekanism transports:** every tier up to Ultimate (cables, pipes, tubes, transporters, thermodynamic conductors) is sold without Data Center's `shop.arcadeCatalog`. The arcade catalog's solar panels stay hidden.

## Config
New section `[shop]` in `data_center_addon-common.toml`:
- `mekanismTiers` (default `true`): sell every Mekanism transport tier.
- `acUnits` (default `true`): sell the AC Indoor and Outdoor Unit.
