# Data Center AddOn 1.5.0

Your data center feels more alive: clients offer contracts, switches show their traffic, and Jade tells you what a block is doing when you look at it.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**.

## Contracts
The Hosting Manager has a new **Contracts** tab. Clients such as a local bakery, a logistics group or a private bank ask you to keep a number of places of one product free for a number of in-game days.

- **Offers:** up to 3 at a time. A new one arrives every in-game day, and each stays open for 4 days.
- **Size and pay** grow with the customer class your reputation has unlocked. Hobby clients want 5–15 places for 10–20 days, the finance sector 100–250 places for 30–60 days. Products you already sell are offered four times as often.
- **Daily check:** once a second the add-on checks whether enough places are free. Free means capacity minus customers over all your racks, the same numbers Data Center uses. A day counts when the places were free for 80 % of it, and it is paid. Otherwise the client gives a strike.
- **The end:** at 3 strikes the contract fails. You pay a penalty of 5 days' pay and lose reputation. Finish it and you get a bonus (half the total pay) and gain reputation.
- **Controls:** "Accept" starts a contract at once. "Cancel" needs a second click to confirm, and cancelling counts as failing. Hover over a row to see all the details. Accepting, strikes, completion and failure also show up as a chat message.
- **Config:** two new entries under `hosting` in `data_center_addon-common.toml`: `contracts` (on/off) and `contractPayMultiplier` (0.1–10, default 1.0).

The Hosting Manager window is now at least 380 pixels wide, so that four tabs fit.

## Switch LEDs
Switches light the LEDs painted on their front:
- **Port LEDs:** green, flickering more the more traffic flows. Amber when the network runs at its limit. Dark without power.
- **Status LED (bottom right):** green, amber at the limit, blinking red without power.
- **Sound:** a short beep when a switch loses or regains power.

## Jade tooltips
With Jade installed, looking at a block shows:
- **Hosting Rack:** product, customers against capacity, income per day, and warnings such as "Licence > income". Only for racks you are allowed to control.
- **Switch:** backplane, power draw, network load in %, and warnings for missing power or a full network.
- **Data cable:** speed and reach, the run's length against its reach, the slowest cable on the run and the speed you actually get. A run that is too long is flagged.
- **Hosting Manager:** racks, customers, income per day, running contracts and contracts without enough free places.

Every tooltip can be switched off in Jade's plugin settings. Without Jade the add-on works as before.

## Languages
English and German.

## Known limitations
- Free places are counted over all your racks, not per Hosting Manager network.
- After the server was off, missed days are caught up (up to 60). Each counts as met or missed depending on the last check before the shutdown.
