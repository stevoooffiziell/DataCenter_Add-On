# Data Center AddOn 1.7.1

Part 2 of the new customer model: each account reaches a **share of the market**, its potential. More reputation means more potential, and a product you have offered for a while is better known.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Reachable share
For every product: **potential = market × share**. The share has three parts:
- **Base:** 20 % of the market. At 0 reputation and fully known, the potential equals the number of customers Data Center's own customer book settles at.
- **Reputation:** ×1 at 0 points up to ×3 at 1000 points, the same reach Data Center uses for its orders.
- **Awareness:** ×0.5 for a product nobody knows you for, up to ×1.
  - It grows while you offer places of the product (63 % of the way in 2 game months).
  - It fades slowly while you offer none (63 % lost in 6 game months).
  - Products you already have customers of when you update start fully known.

Example, shared web 2003 (market 3,250): at 0 reputation and fully known 650 customers are reachable, at 500 reputation 1,300, at 1000 reputation 1,950.

In this release the potential is only shown. Customers still arrive the way Data Center does it; that changes in the next release.

## Market tab
- New column **Reachable**. Hover over it to see how it comes together: base, reputation, awareness.
- The share bar shows your customers inside the reachable part of the market.
