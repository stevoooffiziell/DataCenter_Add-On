# Data Center AddOn 1.12.0

**Delivery orders with rarities.** Like the hosting contracts, every delivery order now has a rarity, coloured like the computer parts, from grey Common to orange Legendary. The better your customer class, the more often rare orders come and the more orders you can run at once.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · JEI 19 (for the recipe pages) · Mekanism Generators 10.7 (for the Wind Generator upgrades) · Flux Networks 8 (for the Flux tab)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Delivery rarities
| Rarity | Colour | Goods | Pay | Reputation | Extra days |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | grey | ×1.0 | ×1.0 | ×1 | +0 |
| Uncommon | green | ×1.15 | ×1.25 | ×1.5 | +1 |
| Rare | blue | ×1.3 | ×1.6 | ×2 | +1 |
| Epic | purple | ×1.5 | ×2.2 | ×3 | +2 |
| Legendary | orange | ×1.75 | ×3.2 | ×5 | +3 |

- The chances are the same as for hosting contracts: from 62 % Common and 1 % Legendary for Hobbyists to 25 % Common and 7 % Legendary for Critical Infrastructure.
- A failed or cancelled order costs the reputation it would have brought, so rare orders are riskier too.
- The simple orders of a young company get a rarity too; it changes their pay, reputation and time, not the amount of goods.
- Delivery orders from older saves count as Common.

## More orders with more reputation
- Running orders: 3 up to 7, waiting offers: 3 up to 6, the same as for hosting contracts. A young company still gets at least 4 offers.

## Hosting Manager
- Every delivery order shows a bar in its rarity's colour, and the client's name is written in it. The detail view names the rarity.
- Hover over the *Running deliveries* header to see the places, the customer class of the next one and the chances for each rarity.

## Guide
- The *Contracts* quest says that delivery orders have the same rarities and places.
