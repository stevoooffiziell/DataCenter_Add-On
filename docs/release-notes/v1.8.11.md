# Data Center AddOn 1.8.11

Part 12 of the new reputation model: **SLA months.** A month with 99.9 % uptime brings renown, once per month instead of a little every hour.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## SLA fulfilment
- The add-on counts the uptime of all your hosting racks per **in-game calendar month** (the share of servers and colocation customers that ran).
- At the end of the month:
  - **uptime ≥ 99.9 %:** **+10 renown** and the message "SLA met: 99.95 % uptime last month. +10 renown."
  - below: a grey note "SLA missed: 99.40 % uptime last month (99.9 % needed)." No penalty, the Ruf already shows the downtime.
- Months without any service are skipped.

## Reputation tab
- Under the Ruf: the uptime of the current month so far, green from 99.9 %. Hover for the rule.
- New source **SLA months** in the renown list.
