# Data Center AddOn 1.2.0

Tiered tooltips that show at a glance which parts are better, a list of the parts built into every server, a wider Shop, and real mainboards for every CPU socket.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
Install on the **server and every client**.

## Tiered tooltips
- Every part is ranked **Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic or Legendary** against all parts of its kind, including Data Center's own parts and those from other data packs.
- Parts are only compared with parts measured the same way: CPUs and GPUs by compute (mining-only cards by hashrate), RAM by size, storage by capacity, PSUs by usable output, fans by airflow, mainboards by their slots.
- Tooltips get a **border and background in the tier colour**, a "◆ Epic" line under the name and a thin divider.
- Works everywhere a part shows a tooltip, including the Shop.

## Built-in components on servers
- Server tooltips now list their **built-in components**: mainboard, CPUs, GPUs, RAM, storage, PSUs, fans and risers, each with a small icon.
- Identical parts are grouped ("4× …"), part names are coloured by tier, and broken parts are marked in red with ✖.
- The server itself gets a tier from the average of its parts (CPUs and GPUs count double).

## Wider Shop
- The **Shop, Sell and Wallet** pages are **140 px wider** by default, so long part names and stats fit.
- Adjustable under *Client → Shop → Extra Width* (0–1000 px; 0 = Data Center's own size). It never grows wider than your screen.

## Real mainboards for every socket
- **46 new real boards**: 41 mainboards (e.g. ASUS P2B, ABIT BP6, ECS K7S5A, ABIT NF7-S, Tyan Thunder, EVGA Classified SR-2, ASUS X99-Deluxe) and 5 mining boards (ASRock H61/H81/H510 Pro BTC, ASRock AB350 Pro4, Biostar TB360-BTC PRO). The add-on now has **711 real parts**.
- **Every CPU socket** now has a real board from the release of its first CPU on.
- With `hideOriginalParts` on, **Data Center's mainboards and mining boards are now hidden too**. Existing configs that still have the old default `hiddenCategories` list are updated automatically. Lists you changed yourself stay as they are.

## Fixes
- Hidden parts no longer post **"New in the Shop"** messages in chat on their release date.

## Settings
New in *Mods → Data Center AddOn → Config → Client*:
- **Tooltips:** `tieredTooltips` (tier colours and tier line), `serverComponents` (component list on servers)
- **Shop:** `extraWidth`

## Languages
English and German.

## Known limitations
- Tiers are relative: a part's tier depends on which parts are installed, so data packs that add parts can shift it.
- Data Center's 15- and 20-slot mining boards have no real counterpart. With the originals hidden, the largest mining board is the ASUS B250 Mining Expert (19 GPUs).
- Release dates, prices and slot counts of the new boards are approximate.
- The wider Shop relies on Data Center 2.1.3's screen layout. If a later Data Center version changes it, the Shop falls back to its normal width.
