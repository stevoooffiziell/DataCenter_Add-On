# Data Center AddOn 1.8.7

Part 8 of the new reputation model: **fixing failures fast pays off.** A failure you fix within a few hours brings a little renown, not only a dent in the Ruf.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Quick fix
- A **failure** begins when servers (or colocation customers) of a rack are down: no power, a crash, a missing part.
- When the rack is back to full service **within 6 game hours** and serves customers again, you get **+1 renown** and a chat message: "Rack at 120, 64, −30 is back in 2.5 game hours. Customers noticed: +1 renown."
- Blips under half a game hour do not count.
- At most one bonus per rack every 3 game days.
- The downtime still counts in the Ruf as before. The bonus is a small reward for reacting, not a way around it.

## Reputation tab
New source **Failures fixed fast** in the renown list.
