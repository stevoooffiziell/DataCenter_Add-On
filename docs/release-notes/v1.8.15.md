# Data Center AddOn 1.8.15

Part 16 of the new reputation model: **reputation per product**, as an option. A good game server Ruf then helps little with enterprise colocation.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Ruf per product (off by default)
- With `rufPerProduct = true` every product also gets **a Ruf of its own**: the same formula as the company's Ruf (5 × downtime, ½ overload, ½ throttling, see 1.8.2), averaged over about 30 game days of that product's racks only.
- The share of a product's market you reach (1.8.2) then follows **half your company's Ruf and half the product's Ruf**.
- Until a product has been measured, the company's Ruf counts alone.
- The customer class (1.8.4) still comes from the company's Ruf.
- Off (default): one Ruf for the whole company, as before.

## Market tab
With the option on, the tooltip of the cancellations column shows the product's own Ruf.

## Config
New option in the `reputation` section:

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `rufPerProduct` | false | A product's market follows half the company's and half the product's own Ruf |
