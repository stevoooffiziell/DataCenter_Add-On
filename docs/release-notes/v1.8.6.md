# Data Center AddOn 1.8.6

Part 7 of the new reputation model: **new customers bring renown once their first month went well.** A new customer who gets 30 days without a disruption talks about you.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## A good first month
- Every customer above the most you ever had (all products together) starts a **first month of 30 game days**.
- **A disruption** is a day with more than 1 % downtime or with a crash. It spoils the first month of every new customer who is in it at the time.
- After 30 days without a disruption these customers bring **+0.1 renown each** (+1 per 10 customers).
- This replaces the immediate +1 per 20 new customers of 1.8.3: renown now comes later, but twice as much, and only for good service.
- Customers who come back after an outage do not start a new first month.

## Reputation tab
Under the sources: how many new customers are in an undisturbed first month and the renown they will bring.
