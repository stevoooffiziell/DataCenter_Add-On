# Data Center AddOn 1.6.3

The Racks tab of the Hosting Manager now shows the places a rack offers, so changing its fill shows up right away.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**.

## Places instead of capacity
Data Center works with two figures for shared hosting and VPS:
- **Capacity:** how many customers the rack's hardware can serve, from its CPUs, memory and drives.
- **Places offered:** capacity × fill. This is how many customers the rack takes in.

Before, the **Customers** column showed "customers / capacity". The fill did not change it.

Now it shows **"customers / places offered"**:
- At 50 % fill, a rack with a capacity of 245 shows "0 / 122".
- Above 100 % fill the figure turns **amber**, because the rack is overbooked.
- **Hover** over a rack row to see the places offered, the fill, the hardware capacity and, when overbooked, a warning that customers above the capacity pay less and complain.

The load bar in the Overview still compares customers with the hardware capacity, so it keeps showing when racks run at their limit. Dedicated servers and colocation have no fill and are unchanged.
