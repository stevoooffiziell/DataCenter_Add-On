# Data Center AddOn 1.9.3

Part 4 of the add-on's industry: the **Press**. It makes plastic parts, circuit board blanks and silicon wafers, the parts the next machines put together.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Press
In the Shop (Infrastructure, $9,000). Violet front.

| Inputs | Output | Time | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 × Plastic pellets | 2 × **Plastic parts** | 3 s | 25 FE/t |
| Copper foil + glass | **Circuit board blank** | 5 s | 35 FE/t |
| Silicon | 2 × **Silicon wafers** | 6 s | 45 FE/t |

Like all factory machines: two inputs and two outputs, a 20,000 FE buffer, automation from any side, the recipe list under the **?**. The two inputs of the circuit board blank may lie in either input slot; with hoppers each slot fills with one of them.

## Where the materials come from
- Plastic pellets: Purchase Terminal (1.9.0)
- Copper foil: Rolling Mill (1.9.2)
- Glass and silicon: Smelter (1.9.1)

## What they are for
- **Plastic parts:** capacitors and connectors (1.9.4), cables (1.9.7)
- **Circuit board blank:** etched into circuit boards (1.9.4)
- **Silicon wafers:** microchips on the chip production line (1.9.5)

## Languages
