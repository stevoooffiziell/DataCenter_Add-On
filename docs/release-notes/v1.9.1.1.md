# Data Center AddOn 1.9.1.1

A layout fix: the **Purchase Terminal** screen is no longer cramped.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Purchase Terminal screen
- **Wider window, two columns** instead of everything stacked:
  - **Left:** a table with one row per raw material: icon, name, price, the buy buttons (+1, +stack) and the auto-reorder target (‹ 64 ›), under column titles.
  - **Right:** the terminal's stock and your inventory, each with a title.
- The budget line with its buttons has the whole width at the top.
- Larger buttons, more space between the rows. Hover over a name for the price per item.
- No change to how the terminal works.

## Languages
English and German.
