# Data Center AddOn 1.7.11

Part 12 of the new customer model: **multiplayer markets**. On a server with several hosting companies, the players can now share each product's pool and compete for it through reputation and price. It is off by default.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Shared pool
- **Off (default):** every account has the whole pool of every product to itself, as before.
- **On (`sharedPool = true`):** the accounts that sell a product share its pool. Each keeps **2s / (s + S)** of its potential:
  - `s` is the account's own share of the pool (base, competition, reputation, customer classes, awareness, price),
  - `S` is the summed share of all accounts active in the product, the account itself included.

| Situation | Each keeps |
|---|---|
| Alone in the market | ×1.00 |
| Two equally strong accounts | ×0.67 |
| Three equally strong accounts | ×0.50 |
| One twice as strong as the other | ×0.80 (stronger) and ×0.50 (weaker) |

- An account counts as active in a product while it offers places of it or has customers of it.
- So a better reputation, a higher class or a lower price now also takes customers from the other players. Customers above a shrinking potential leave over the months (1.7.8).
- Accounts are Data Center's wallet accounts: players who share a wallet count as one company.
- The fictional competitors (1.7.10) stay on top, for all players alike.

## Market tab
With the shared pool on, the tooltip of **Reachable** shows with how many accounts the pool is shared and the factor.

## Config
New option in the `market` section:

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `sharedPool` | false | Accounts selling the same product share its pool |
