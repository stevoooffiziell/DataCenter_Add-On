# Data Center AddOn 1.7.12

Part 13 and the last of the new customer model: **the market at a glance**. One line tells you how big a market is, how much of it you reach, how many customers you have and what the next customer class would bring.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Market summary
- For example: **"Market Shared Web: 4.2K · reachable 900 · you have 310 · next class (Mid-Market): +400"**.
- **Next class:** how many more customers you could reach once you unlock the next customer class: its reputation threshold and its sub-pool (1.7.4), with everything else as it is now. At the highest class the line says so.
- **Market tab:** the line sits under the table. It follows the product under the mouse, otherwise the product with the most customers.
- **Overview:** hover over a product's name in the table to see its line.

## The new customer model in short (1.7.0 to 1.7.12)
For shared hosting, VPS and colocation:
- **Market (1.7.0):** every product has a pool of potential customers that grows with the internet.
- **Potential (1.7.1, 1.7.4, 1.7.9, 1.7.10, 1.7.11):** pool × market events × competition × 20 % × reputation (×1 to ×2) × awareness (×0.5 to ×1) × customer classes (×1 to ×2.05) × price, shared with other players if `sharedPool` is on.
- **New customers (1.7.2, 1.7.3):** 25 % of the gap to the potential per month, plus word of mouth.
- **Cancellations (1.7.5):** 1 % a month with good service, up to 8 % with bad.
- **Waiting list (1.7.6):** customers without a free place wait about 7 days.
- **Segment pools (1.7.7):** game launches and video platforms grow their segments.
- **Market events (1.7.8):** the dotcom crash and the Web 2.0 boom change the size of the markets, visibly.
- **Competitors (1.7.10):** fictional companies take part of each market, grow and go bankrupt.
- Everything is shown in the **Market** tab of the Hosting Manager. `marketModel = false` brings back Data Center's own customer model.
