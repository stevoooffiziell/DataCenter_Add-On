# Data Center AddOn 1.10.0

**Flux Networks in the shop.** A new partner tab **Flux** under *Partners* sells the devices of Flux Networks, for wireless power from the generators to the server rooms.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · JEI 19 (for the recipe pages) · Mekanism Generators 10.7 (for the Wind Generator upgrades) · Flux Networks 8 (for the Flux tab)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Flux tab
| Item | Price | What for |
|---|---|---|
| Flux Plug | $750 | takes power into the network (at generators or energy cubes) |
| Flux Point | $750 | hands the power out wirelessly, e.g. to a rack or a UPS |
| Flux Controller | $2,500 | charges Flux devices in inventories |
| Basic Flux Storage | $150 | 2 million FE buffer in the network |
| Herculean Flux Storage | $800 | 16 million FE |
| Gargantuan Flux Storage | $4,000 | 128 million FE |
| Flux Configurator | $100 | copies and pastes the settings of Flux devices |

- Like every partner tab, it only shows with Flux Networks installed.
- A Flux Plug or Point moves 800,000 FE/t by default.
