# Data Center AddOn 1.8.3

Part 4 of the new reputation model: **Bekanntheit (renown)**, the second value next to the Ruf. It grows through what you do, and it sets how much of the markets you reach.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Two values instead of one
- **Ruf** (1.8.2): how good your service is, 0–100 %, a sliding value of the last 30 days.
- **Bekanntheit** (new): how well known your company is. It is Data Center's reputation points (0 to 1000), so the points you already have carry over.

## What renown grows from
- **Finished orders:** contracts and delivery orders give their reputation as before (e.g. +3 to +19 for a contract).
- **New customers:** +1 for every 20 customers above the most you ever had (all products together). Customers who come back after an outage do not count again.
- **Milestones:**

| Milestone | Renown |
|---|---|
| 10 / 50 / 100 / 500 / 1,000 customers | +5 / +10 / +20 / +40 / +60 |
| First contract, first delivery | +10 each |
| First service ticket | +5 |

- **Failed or cancelled orders** cost renown as before.
- Running servers give nothing (1.8.0–1.8.2). More sources come in the next releases.

## What renown does
It sets your share of the markets (1.7.1): ×1 at 0 up to ×2 at 1000. The per-product value that grows while you offer places is now called **presence**, so the two are not mixed up.

## Hosting Manager
- **Reputation tab:** on the right, renown with a bar and how much each source brought since this update.
- **Overview:** the first bar is now labelled *Renown*.
- **Market tab:** the tooltip of *Reachable* says *Renown* and *Presence*.
