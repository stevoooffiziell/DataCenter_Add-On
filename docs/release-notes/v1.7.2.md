# Data Center AddOn 1.7.2

Part 3 of the new customer model, and the one that changes the game: **new customers now close the gap** between your reachable potential and the customers you have. A new product fills fast and then levels off, instead of creeping up over years.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Inflow by gap
For shared hosting, VPS and colocation:
- **New customers per month = (potential − customers) × close rate.** The close rate is 25 % a month.
- New customers only take free places, as before. They come in Data Center's segment mix of the year.
- Cancellations stay as in Data Center (about 3 % a month, by segment and year).
- With these numbers a product reaches **63 % of its level in about 3.5 game months** (Data Center: 30+ months) and then levels off at about 90 % of the potential.

Example, shared web 2003 at 0 reputation (potential 650): the first month brings about 160 new customers. Data Center gave 25 a month.

## What Data Center's rack menu shows
"Orders" in the Hosting Rack menu are now the add-on's new customers per month, "Cancellations" stay Data Center's.

## Config
New section `market` in `data_center_addon-common.toml`:

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `marketModel` | true | Use the market model. `false`: customers arrive the way Data Center does it; the Market tab still shows the market. |
| `closeRate` | 0.25 | Share of the gap that becomes customers per game month |

## Technical
The add-on takes over one step of Data Center's customer book (`HostingCustomers.step`). If a Data Center update changes that method, the hook stops silently and Data Center's own model runs again. The game does not crash.
