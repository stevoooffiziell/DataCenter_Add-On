# Data Center AddOn 1.9.19

**The waiting list is back.** New customers come again even when no places are free: they queue up on the waiting list and take the next free places, or give up after `waitDays` in-game days on average. The waiting list has no effect on reputation or renown.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · JEI 19 (for the recipe pages)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Changed
- New customers no longer stop when all places are taken (as before 1.9.18).
- Contract offers still only ask for free places, and an offer can still only be accepted while its places are free.

## Languages
English and German.
