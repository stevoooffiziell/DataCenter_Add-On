# Data Center AddOn 1.7.8

Part 9 of the new customer model: **market events you can see**. The dotcom crash shrinks the markets, the Web 2.0 boom makes them bigger, and the Market tab says so, instead of a hidden factor on the income.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Market events
- Data Center has a market mood for worlds that start before 2009: 1.0 in 1999, up to 1.15 in early 2000, down to 0.63 in 2002/03, back to 0.95 from 2005, a dip to 0.8 after Lehman in 2008. Until now it multiplied the income of every rack, without saying so.
- With the market model on, this mood now changes the **size of the markets** of shared hosting and VPS instead. The income of those racks no longer carries it.
  - Dedicated servers keep the mood on their income, as before (they have no market).
  - Colocation was never affected and still is not.
- **Web 2.0 boom** on top: from October 2004 the markets grow by up to **+12 %** (full from mid-2006 to the end of 2007) and the boost is gone by September 2008.
- **Customers above your potential leave:** when a market shrinks (or you lose a customer class), 10 % of the customers above your potential leave per game month. Before, they stayed until they cancelled the usual way.
- The phases: dotcom boom, dotcom crash, recovery, Web 2.0 boom, financial crisis. Worlds that start in 2009 or later have no market mood, as in Data Center.

Example, shared web in mid-2002 (mood 0.68): the market is about 2,300 instead of 3,350. A company with 800 customers and a potential of 460 loses about 34 customers a month until it is down to its potential.

## Market tab
- The top line names the current event and its factor, e.g. "Market: dotcom crash ×0.68" (amber when the markets shrink, green when they grow). Hover for an explanation.
- The **Market** column already includes the factor; its tooltip shows it.

## Note
Data Center's own Hosting Rack menu still shows its market mood. With the market model on, it no longer changes that rack's income (shared and VPS).

## Technical
The add-on replaces one call in the Hosting Rack's income code (`HostingDemand.factor`). If a Data Center update changes it, the mood scales the income again. The game does not crash.
