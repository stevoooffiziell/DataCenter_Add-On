# Data Center AddOn 1.8.9

Part 10 of the new reputation model: **customer reviews.** Now and then a customer writes a review, good or bad, and it moves your renown.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Reviews
- On about **one game day in three** one of your customers writes a review (if you have customers).
- The customer is picked as your customers are spread: a product with many customers is reviewed more often, and the segment (online shop, game server, company website …) follows that product's customers.
- **Good or bad** depends on the quality of that product's service (downtime, overload, throttling of the last 3 game days, see 1.7.5): about **90 %** good reviews with perfect service, down to **10 %** with bad service. A product not measured yet gets 75 %.
- A good review gives **+3 renown**, a bad one costs **3**.
- Each review comes as a chat message, e.g. "★★★★★ Review by a customer (Online Shop, Shared Web): "Finally a host that simply works." +3 renown".

## Reputation tab
- New source **Customer reviews** in the renown list.
- The last review with its stars, segment and age; hover to read it.
