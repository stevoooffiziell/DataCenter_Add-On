# Data Center AddOn 1.11.0

**Hosting contracts with rarities.** Every contract now has a rarity, coloured like the computer parts, from grey Common to orange Legendary. The better your customer class, the more often rare contracts come and the more contracts you can run at once. **Service tickets are gone.**

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · JEI 19 (for the recipe pages) · Mekanism Generators 10.7 (for the Wind Generator upgrades) · Flux Networks 8 (for the Flux tab)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Contract rarities
| Rarity | Colour | Places | Pay | Reputation | Penalty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | grey | ×1.0 | ×1.0 | ×1 | ×1.0 |
| Uncommon | green | ×1.15 | ×1.25 | ×1.5 | ×1.1 |
| Rare | blue | ×1.3 | ×1.6 | ×2 | ×1.25 |
| Epic | purple | ×1.5 | ×2.2 | ×3 | ×1.5 |
| Legendary | orange | ×1.75 | ×3.2 | ×5 | ×2.0 |

- A contract still never wants more places than are free.
- A failed contract costs twice the reputation it would bring, so rare contracts are riskier too.
- Mini orders are always Common; emergency orders (⚡) roll their rarity like any other.
- Contracts from older saves count as Common.

## Hosting Manager
- Every contract shows a bar in its rarity's colour, and the client's name is written in it. The tooltip names the rarity.
- Hover over the *Running contracts* header to see how many contracts can run, the customer class of the next place, and today's chances for each rarity.

## Removed
- Service tickets: no more requests to right-click a rack, no ticket list in the *Startup* tab, no *First service ticket* milestone, no `serviceTickets` config option, no *Service tickets* quest. Open tickets in older saves simply disappear.

## Guide
- The *Contracts* quest explains the rarities and the extra places.
