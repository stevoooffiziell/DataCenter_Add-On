# Data Center AddOn 1.9.9

Part 10 and the last of the add-on's industry: the **Factory Manager**, a terminal of its own for your factory, separate from the Hosting Manager. It shows what your factory costs and earns, and what every machine around it is doing.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Factory Manager
In the Shop (Infrastructure, $2,500). Data Center's mining dashboard with an orange screen. It needs no cable: it sees everything within **32 blocks** (loaded chunks).

### Overview tab
- **Cards:**
  - **Budget** in your Purchase Terminals around the manager
  - **Spent** on raw materials in the last 30 game days
  - **Revenue** at the Sales Terminals in the last 30 game days
  - **Margin** = revenue − raw materials (power and machines not counted), green or red
  - **Today:** today's revenue minus today's purchases
- **Machines:** every factory machine around the manager (Smelter, Rolling Mill, Wire Drawer, Press, Assembler, chip production line, Hardware Fab): what it makes, its state and its power, with its position. Scroll with the mouse wheel.
  - **Working** (green), **Idle (no input)** (grey), **No power** (red), **Blocked** (amber: output full, chip line not formed, part not on sale).

### Trade tab
- **Sales** per product over the last 30 game days: units, revenue, the price now and the demand of your market.
- **Purchases** per raw material over the last 30 game days: units, spent and the price now.
- Spending and revenue count every terminal of your company, wherever it stands; the budget and the machines only those around the manager.

## Statistics
From this version the add-on records what each company buys at its Purchase Terminals and sells at its Sales Terminals, per game day, for the last 30 days. Purchases and sales before the update are not in it.

## The industry in short (1.9.0 to 1.9.9)
- **Purchase Terminal** (1.9.0): raw materials with era prices, budget, auto-reorder.
- **Machines** (1.9.1–1.9.4): Smelter, Rolling Mill, Wire Drawer, Press, Assembler.
- **Chip production line** (1.9.5): multiblock, speed by lithography units.
- **Sales Terminal** (1.9.6): market prices with saturation.
- **Own use** (1.9.7): cables, switches and delivery orders from components.
- **Hardware Fab** (1.9.8): any server part on sale today from components.
- **Factory Manager** (1.9.9).
