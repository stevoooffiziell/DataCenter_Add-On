# Data Center AddOn 1.9.10

**JEI integration.** With JEI installed, every recipe and price of the add-on's industry can be looked up there: press R on an item to see how it is made, U to see what it is used for.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · **JEI 19 (for the recipe pages)**
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Recipe pages
- **One page per factory machine:** Smelter, Rolling Mill, Wire Drawer, Press, Assembler. Inputs with their amounts, the output, time and power at speed 1.
- **Chip production line:** wafer, gold contacts and redstone to microchips, with the note that each lithography unit makes it faster. The controller, the lithography unit and the cleanroom wall are shown as its stations.
- **Hardware Fab:** one page per kind of part (CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, power supply, board, fan). It shows the components the kind uses (hover for their share of the material value) and cycles through every part of that kind. The material is about half the part's Shop price, so there are no fixed amounts.
- **Purchase Terminal:** every raw material it sells, with its price in 1999 and how the price moves (copper, gold, oil or inflation).
- **Sales Terminal:** every product it buys, with its price in 1999.
- Each machine and terminal is registered as the station of its page: press U on the block to see everything it does.

## Info pages
For the cleanroom wall and lithography unit (how the chip production line is built), the Factory Manager and plastic pellets.

## Technical
JEI is optional. The add-on only compiles against JEI's API; without JEI nothing changes.
