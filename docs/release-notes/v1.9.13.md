# Data Center AddOn 1.9.13

**Hosting Manager in the starter kit.** Data Center's starter kit now also holds the add-on's Hosting Manager, so you can watch customers, orders and your reputation from the start.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · JEI 19 (for the recipe pages)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Starter kit
- The Hosting Manager comes with the kit: on first join, on a new island or by the kit command. Each player gets it once, together with the kit.
- With a full inventory it drops at your feet.
- Players who already received their kit do not get it afterwards; it is in the Shop as before.

## SkyBlock starter map (separate download)
`tools/generate-skyblock.py` builds a datapack, `DataCenter-SkyBlock.zip`: add it under "Data Packs" when creating a world. The overworld becomes a void, and on the first load a small island is placed at 0, 0 with an oak, a 7 × 7 server room built from Data Center's dark blocks, and a chest with lava, ice, seeds, building blocks and a German island guide.

## Languages
English and German.
