# Data Center AddOn 1.9.2

Part 3 of the add-on's industry: two more machines, the **Rolling Mill** and the **Wire Drawer**. They turn ingots into the first electronics materials: copper foil, gold contacts and copper wire.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Rolling Mill
In the Shop (Infrastructure, $8,000). Steel-blue front.

| Input | Output | Time | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper ingot | 2 × **Copper foil** | 4 s | 25 FE/t |
| Gold ingot | 4 × **Gold contacts** | 5 s | 30 FE/t |

## Wire Drawer
In the Shop (Infrastructure, $7,000). Copper-coloured front.

| Input | Output | Time | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper ingot | 4 × **Copper wire** | 3 s | 20 FE/t |

Both work like the Smelter (1.9.1): two inputs and two outputs, a 20,000 FE buffer, automation from any side, the recipe list under the **?**.

## What the new materials are for
- **Copper foil:** circuit board blanks and capacitors (1.9.3, 1.9.4)
- **Gold contacts:** connectors and microchips (1.9.4, 1.9.5)
- **Copper wire:** cables (1.9.7)

A full line now: Purchase Terminal (raw copper) → Smelter → Rolling Mill / Wire Drawer.
