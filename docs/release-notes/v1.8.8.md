# Data Center AddOn 1.8.8

Part 9 of the new reputation model: **certifications make you known.** The first time you run a rack in a room of a higher tier, your renown gets a large one-time boost.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Certification boost

| Tier reached | Renown |
|---|---|
| Tier I | +25 |
| Tier II | +50 |
| Tier III | +100 |
| Tier IV | +150 |

- It counts once per account and tier, as soon as a rack in such a room serves customers. Skipping a tier gives both boosts.
- A chat message tells you: "Your first rack in a Tier II room: the trade press reports. +50 renown."
- The tier your racks already have when you update counts as reached, without a boost.
- Together with the class requirements (1.8.4) a certification now helps twice: it opens the higher classes for your racks and brings the renown they need.

## Reputation tab
New source **Certifications** in the renown list.
