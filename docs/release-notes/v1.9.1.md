# Data Center AddOn 1.9.1

Part 2 of the add-on's industry: **factory machines**, starting with the **Smelter**. It turns ores into ingots faster than a furnace and makes the first material of the electronics chain: **silicon**.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Factory machines
All machines of the 1.9.x releases work the same way:
- **Two input and two output slots.** The inputs of a recipe may lie in either input slot.
- **Power (FE):** a buffer of 20,000 FE, filled from any side (up to 1,000 FE per tick). While working the machine draws the recipe's power every tick. Without power it pauses and keeps its progress.
- **Automation:** hoppers, pipes and other mods put items in from any side; they only go into the input slots, and only items one of the machine's recipes uses. Taking items out (from any side) only takes from the output slots. Each input slot holds one kind, so two-input recipes get both inputs.
- The machine glows while it works.
- **Screen:** power bar, progress, inputs and outputs. The **?** in the corner lists the machine's recipes with time and power.

## Smelter
In the Shop (Infrastructure, $6,000).

| Inputs | Output | Time | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw copper | Copper ingot | 3 s | 20 FE/t |
| Raw iron | Iron ingot | 3 s | 20 FE/t |
| Raw gold | Gold ingot | 3 s | 20 FE/t |
| Sand | Glass | 2 s | 15 FE/t |
| Quartz + coal | **Silicon** (new item) | 6 s | 40 FE/t |

A furnace takes 10 s per ore. Together with the Purchase Terminal (1.9.0) a hopper chain now runs on its own: terminal → hopper → smelter → hopper → chest.

## Config
New options in the `industry` section:

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `machineSpeed` | 1.0 | Speed of the factory machines (2.0 = twice as fast and twice the power per tick) |
| `machineEnergy` | 1.0 | Multiplier for the power they draw |
