# Data Center AddOn 1.1.0

An add-on for **Data Center** that adds real computer hardware, an in-game overlay with the date, your wallet and crypto prices, and a server room panel that carries power.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
Install on the **server and every client**.

## Real computer parts
- **665 real parts** in the Shop, from early-2000s Athlons and Pentiums up to Ryzen 9000, Core Ultra, EPYC, RTX 50 series, H100/B200 and MI300X. Includes CPUs, GPUs (including Intel Arc and workstation/data-center cards), RAM, SSDs and HDDs, PSUs, mainboards and fans.
- Every part has its **real release date and launch price** (MSRP). It only appears in the Shop once the in-game date reaches its release, just like Data Center's own parts.
- Compute and mining speeds follow Data Center's existing balance.
- **Optional: hide the original parts.** `hideOriginalParts` in `config/data_center_addon-common.toml` (default `false`) removes Data Center's fictional-brand parts from the Shop. Parts you already own keep working. `hiddenCategories` controls which shop categories are affected; mainboards and fans are kept by default. Takes effect the next time a world is loaded.
- **Hosting boost:** this add-on's storage parts get **+10% capacity** while their server runs in a Hosting Rack (`storageBoost`, 1.0–2.0).
- 16 new item textures in Data Center's pixel-art style for Intel Core Ultra, Ryzen X3D, Xeon, Intel Arc, workstation and SXM cards, ECC RAM, NVMe heatsinks, enterprise HDDs, SATA SSDs, PSUs and Noctua fans.
- Shop tabs renamed from the parody brands to **AMD, Intel, NVIDIA and Radeon/Arc**.

## Overlay: date, wallet and crypto prices
- Shows the **in-game date**, your **wallet balance**, what your **coins are worth**, and the **price of every coin** with its change since the previous day (▲ green / ▼ red).
- **Fully configurable, Jade-style:** position (corners, edges, centre), offset, scale, box style (tooltip / flat / none), opacity, colours, text shadow, date format, which coins to show and in what order, change period, hide in menus or with F3.
- Edit everything in game under *Mods → Data Center AddOn → Config → Client* (changes apply instantly), or in `config/data_center_addon-client.toml`.
- Press **O** to show or hide it (rebindable under *Controls → Data Center*).

## Powered Server Room Panel
- A wall block that **seals a server room** like any other wall and **passes FE straight through** between its two ends, so you can bring power into a sealed room without a gap.

## Languages
English and German.

## Known limitations
- Release dates for some PSUs, RAM kits, drives and boards are approximate (month level).
- Intel Arc GPUs are listed under the Radeon/Arc tab, because Data Center has no Intel GPU category.
- On a dedicated server, players should use the same `hideOriginalParts` settings as the server.
