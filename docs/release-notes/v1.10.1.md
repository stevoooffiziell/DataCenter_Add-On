# Data Center AddOn 1.10.1

**Flux Networks gets dearer over time.** The devices of the Flux tab cost several times as much as before, and their prices now rise with the game date instead of falling like computer parts. Wireless power stays a late-game luxury.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · JEI 19 (for the recipe pages) · Mekanism Generators 10.7 (for the Wind Generator upgrades) · Flux Networks 8 (for the Flux tab)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Flux prices
Until the end of 2008 the base price applies; from then on every device costs **18 % more per year**, rising day by day.

| Item | Before | 2009 | 2014 | 2019 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flux Plug | $750 | $2,500 | $5,700 | $13,100 | $41,700 |
| Flux Point | $750 | $2,500 | $5,700 | $13,100 | $41,700 |
| Flux Controller | $2,500 | $7,500 | $17,200 | $39,300 | $125,100 |
| Basic Flux Storage | $150 | $600 | $1,400 | $3,100 | $10,000 |
| Herculean Flux Storage | $800 | $3,000 | $6,900 | $15,700 | $50,000 |
| Gargantuan Flux Storage | $4,000 | $15,000 | $34,300 | $78,500 | $250,200 |
| Flux Configurator | $100 | $250 | $570 | $1,300 | $4,200 |

- Before, Data Center wrote the Flux devices down by 30 % a year like computer parts, so in the late game they cost only a fraction of their list price.
- Data Center's global price multiplier still applies.
- Selling back stays capped at what you paid, so buying early and selling late brings no profit.
