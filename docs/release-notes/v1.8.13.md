# Data Center AddOn 1.8.13

Part 14 of the new reputation model: **incidents have an aftermath.** A crash no longer takes 20 points once; it presses your Ruf for 30 days.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Aftermath of a crash
- Data Center takes **20 reputation points** for every crash. With the reputation model on, that is gone: renown is not lost by a crash any more.
- Instead a crash is a **serious incident**: it takes **8 % of the Ruf** at once, and the dent fades out evenly over **30 game days** (4 % left after 15 days).
- Several crashes add up. Three crashes in one week cost the Ruf about 22 %, enough to lose a class (1.8.4).
- This replaces the flat −2 % per crash of 1.8.2.
- The customers a crash costs (Data Center's outage loss) stay as before.

## Reputation tab
The crash line shows what the incidents still take from the Ruf, e.g. "Crashes 2 (−11.7 %)".
