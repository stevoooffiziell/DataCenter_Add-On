# Data Center AddOn 1.7.9

Part 10 of the new customer model: **set your own prices**. Per product you choose between 30 % below and 50 % above the list price. Cheaper brings more customers and less per customer, dearer the opposite.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Price per product
- In the Market tab every product has a price from **−30 % to +50 %** in steps of 10 %. It starts at ±0 %.
- **What customers pay:** the rack income of the product changes by the same percentage (shared hosting, VPS and colocation).
- **How many you reach:** each 10 % cheaper raises your reachable share by 15 %, each 10 % dearer lowers it by 15 %.

| Price | Pay | Reachable | Income at a full potential |
|---|---|---|---|
| −30 % | ×0.70 | ×1.45 | ×1.02 |
| −10 % | ×0.90 | ×1.15 | ×1.04 |
| ±0 % | ×1.00 | ×1.00 | ×1.00 |
| +20 % | ×1.20 | ×0.70 | ×0.84 |
| +50 % | ×1.50 | ×0.25 | ×0.38 |

- What that means: with plenty of places, a lower price brings about the same money from **more** customers (more hardware, more word of mouth). When your places are the limit and customers are waiting, a higher price earns more per place.
- Setup fees and annual plans (founding phase) follow your price.
- Customers above a lower potential leave over the months (see 1.7.8), so raising the price a lot slowly costs customers.
- Prices only exist with the market model on (`marketModel = true`).

## Market tab
- The last column is now **Price** with ◀ ▶ buttons. Hover over the value to see what customers pay and what it does to your reachable share.
- The market share moved into the tooltip of **Customers**.
- The tooltip of **Reachable** shows the price factor.

## Technical
The add-on scales the per-customer income where the Hosting Rack reads it (`HostingModeRates.unitNet`, `coloUsdPerServer`). If a Data Center update changes these calls, prices stop changing the income. The game does not crash.
