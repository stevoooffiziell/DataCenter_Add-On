# Data Center AddOn 1.9.6

Part 7 of the add-on's industry: the **Sales Terminal**. It buys the materials and components your factory makes and pays into your wallet. With it the chain from raw material to money is complete.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Sales Terminal
In the Shop (Infrastructure, $5,000). Like the Purchase Terminal, with a green screen.
- **Inbox** of 9 slots: put products in by hand or let **hoppers and pipes** fill it from any side. Only the products below are taken; nothing comes out.
- Once a second everything in the inbox is **sold** and paid into the wallet of the company that placed the terminal.
- The screen shows the price list with the current price and demand of every product, and what this terminal has sold so far.

- Intermediate materials pay about 1.5 to 2 times their raw materials, finished components 3 to 6 times. Power and the machines are your costs.
- **Over the years** the prices follow what the product is made of: the gold share follows the gold market, the copper share the copper market, the plastic share oil, the rest inflation. A rising gold price does not eat the margin of connectors and chips.

## Market saturation
Like Data Center's timber market, per company and product:
- The more you sell of one product, the less the buyers pay for the next one. After **$5,000** (at full price) sold recently, the price is halfway down to the floor; it never falls below **30 %**.
- The sold amount fades by half every **7 game days**, so demand comes back.
- A sale is paid along the curve: one big batch or many small ones bring the same money.
- Selling several different products is worth it: each has its own demand.

## Config
New options in the `industry` section:

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `salesPrices` | 1.0 | Multiplier for the sales prices |
| `salesHalfLifeUsd` | 5000 | Value sold after which the price is halfway to the floor |
| `salesRecoveryDays` | 7.0 | Game days for the sold amount to fade by half |
| `salesDemandFloor` | 0.3 | Lowest share of the price a saturated market pays |
