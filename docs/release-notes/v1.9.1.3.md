# Data Center AddOn 1.9.1.3

**The hotbar in Data Center's look**, to match the restyled screens of 1.9.1.2.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Tech-panel hotbar
- A dark panel with a border and blue corner accents instead of Minecraft's grey bar.
- Every slot has a dark frame; the **selected slot** is outlined in blue and slightly lit.
- The **off-hand slot** gets the same look, on the side Minecraft puts it (depends on your main hand).
- **Attack indicator** (when set to "Hotbar" in the video settings): a thin blue bar next to the hotbar instead of the sword icon.
- Same size and place as Minecraft's hotbar: hearts, food, armour and the experience bar stay where they are, and the items keep their bounce when picked up, their count and their durability bar.
- The spectator hotbar stays as it is.

## Config
New option in the client config (`data_center_addon-client.toml`, section `screens`):

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `techHotbar` | true | Draw the hotbar in the tech-panel look; `false` brings back Minecraft's hotbar |

## Technical
The add-on replaces the vanilla hotbar layer (NeoForge's GUI layer event). Mods that draw their own hotbar or change the vanilla one can conflict; turn the option off then.
