# Data Center AddOn 1.9.4

Part 5 of the add-on's industry: the **Assembler**. It makes the first finished components: circuit boards, capacitors and connectors.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Assembler
A pick-and-place machine, in the Shop (Infrastructure, $12,000). Green front.

| Inputs | Output | Time | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| Circuit board blank + redstone | **Circuit board** | 5 s | 40 FE/t |
| Copper foil + plastic parts | 2 × **Capacitors** | 4 s | 30 FE/t |
| 2 × Gold contacts + plastic parts | 2 × **Connectors** | 4 s | 30 FE/t |

Like all factory machines: two inputs and two outputs, a 20,000 FE buffer, automation from any side, the recipe list under the **?**.

## The chain so far

| Component | Made from | Machines |
|---|---|---|
| Circuit board | raw copper, sand, redstone | Smelter → Rolling Mill → Press → Assembler |
| Capacitor | raw copper, plastic pellets | Smelter → Rolling Mill + Press → Assembler |
| Connector | raw gold, plastic pellets | Smelter → Rolling Mill + Press → Assembler |

Circuit boards, capacitors and connectors can be sold at the Sales Terminal (1.9.6) and are used in the add-on's own recipes (1.9.7). Next: microchips on the chip production line (1.9.5).
