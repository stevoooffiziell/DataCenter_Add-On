# Data Center AddOn 1.9.11

**More customer classes.** With the add-on's reputation model, the 5 customer classes become a ladder of **14 classes**, and renown now goes up to **2500** instead of 1000.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips) · JEI 19 (for the recipe pages)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## The classes
| Class | Group | Ruf | Renown | Room | Income |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hobbyists | Private Customers | – | 0 | – | ×1.00 |

| Hyperscalers | Critical Sectors | 99.3 % | 1950 | Tier IV | ×1.88 |
| Critical Infrastructure | Critical Sectors | 99.5 % | 2400 | Tier IV | ×2.00 |

- Every class belongs to one of Data Center's five classes, renamed to groups (Private Customers, Small Companies, Mid-Market, Large Enterprises, Critical Sectors). Data Center only sees the group: its advancements, room checks and the highest class a rack can serve.
- The classes of 1.9.10 keep their requirements; existing worlds start in the class they had.
- **Income:** a rack earns the multiplier of your class, as far as its room and the rack allow. The top three classes need a Tier IV room.
- **Market:** every class opens a sub-pool of its own (15 % of the hobbyist market for the lower classes, down to 5 %), up to ×2.44 together. Above 1000 renown the reach keeps growing, up to ×2.5 at 2500.
- **Orders:** contracts, delivery orders, service tickets and setup fees pay by the finer class, and higher classes bring more renown.
- **Reputation tab:** shows "class X/14"; its tooltip lists every class with what it needs and pays. The Overview shows renown out of 2500.
- A new class is announced with its own message.
- With the reputation model off, nothing changes: Data Center's 5 classes and 1000 points.
