# Data Center AddOn 1.8.14

Part 15 of the new reputation model: **complaints by segment.** Game server customers mind an outage four times as much as company websites, and now the Ruf and the cancellations know it.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Outage sensitivity
- Data Center gives every customer segment an outage sensitivity: company websites and portals 1.0, dotcom, e-mail and Web 2.0 1.2–1.5, online shops 2.5, enterprise 3.0, game servers 4.0.
- **Downtime now counts weighted by the average sensitivity of the product's customers**, relative to 1.5 (about a typical shared web mix):
  - in the **Ruf** (1.8.2),
  - in the **cancellations by quality** (1.7.5) and the chance of a good review (1.8.9),
  - in what counts as a disruption for new customers (1.8.6) and for the SLA (1.8.11).
- Examples: an hour of downtime on a game server rack counts about 2.7 times; on a rack of company websites about 0.7 times.
- Dedicated servers have no customer mix; their downtime counts as before.
- The mix follows your actual customers of the product and is refreshed every few seconds.
