# Data Center AddOn 1.8.17

Part 18 of the new reputation model: **the history as a chart.** The Overview shows how your Ruf and renown went over the last 30 days.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## History chart
- **Overview:** a small chart right of the renown and network bars.
  - **Green:** the Ruf, 0 to 100 %.
  - **Blue:** the renown, scaled to its highest value of the period (at least 100).
  - Hover to see the values at the start and now.
- The add-on records Ruf and renown at the end of every game day and keeps the last 30 days. The chart begins with the first day after this update ("history from tomorrow").
