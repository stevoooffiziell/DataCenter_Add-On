# Data Center AddOn 1.8.18

Part 19 and the last of the new reputation model: **notices when you lose reputation.** A chat message names the rack and what costs your Ruf.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Loss notices
- **Crash:** "−8 % Ruf: rack at 120, 64, −30 crashed. The dent fades over 30 days."
- **Servers down:** "Ruf at risk: servers of the rack at 120, 64, −30 are down. Every hour costs Ruf." when the downtime begins.
- **Throttling:** "Ruf at risk: the rack at 120, 64, −30 runs throttled (heat, attack or a slow network)." when it begins.
- Downtime and throttling notices come at most **once per rack and game day**, so a flickering rack does not flood the chat.
- Lost renown (failed orders, bad reviews) already had its own messages.

## Config
New option in the `reputation` section:

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `lossNotices` | true | Chat notices when a rack costs Ruf |

## The new reputation model in short (1.8.0 to 1.8.18)
- **Runtime is the baseline (1.8.0, 1.8.1):** running servers neither earn nor lose points by the hour.
- **Ruf (1.8.2, 1.8.13, 1.8.14, 1.8.15):** the quality of the last 30 days, 0–100 %: downtime (weighted by how much the customers mind it), overload, throttling and crashes that press it for 30 days. It scales your reachable market share. Optionally per product.
- **Bekanntheit (1.8.3):** Data Center's points, grown only by actions:
  - orders ×3 (1.8.5), new customers with a good first month (1.8.6), quick fixes (1.8.7), certifications (1.8.8), reviews (1.8.9), advertising (1.8.10), SLA months (1.8.11), milestones;
  - slowly forgotten without anything new (1.8.12).
- **Customer class from both (1.8.4):** e.g. Mid-Market needs Ruf 90 % and renown 200, plus Tier I for the rack.
- **Transparency (1.8.16–1.8.18):** Reputation tab, a breakdown line and a 30-day chart in the Overview, notices on losses.
- `reputationModel = false` brings back Data Center's own reputation.
