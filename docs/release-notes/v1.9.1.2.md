# Data Center AddOn 1.9.1.2

**Minecraft's own screens in Data Center's look.** Chests, furnaces, the crafting table and the inventory now use the same dark tech panel as Data Center's machines and the add-on's terminals.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Restyled screens
- **Chests** (also barrels, ender chests, large chests and minecarts with chests), **shulker boxes**
- **Hoppers**, **dispensers** and **droppers**
- **Crafting table**
- **Furnace, blast furnace, smoker:** the flame becomes a bar that rises with the fuel left, the arrow a progress bar
- **Survival inventory:** the player in a dark frame, the crafting arrow redrawn

What changes:
- Dark panel with grid lines and blue corner accents, as in Data Center.
- Every slot gets a dark frame. The slots of the block (chest, furnace …) have a blue corner mark, your own inventory has none.
- Titles in light grey, "Inventory" and "Crafting" in blue, so they stay readable on the dark background.

What stays: slot positions, the recipe book, empty armour slot icons, tooltips, everything that is not drawn on the background. Screens of other mods and the remaining vanilla screens (brewing stand, anvil, enchanting table, villagers …) are not changed.

## Config
New option in the client config (`data_center_addon-client.toml`, section `screens`):

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `techStyle` | true | Draw these screens in the tech-panel look; `false` brings back Minecraft's own textures |

## Technical
The add-on paints over the screens' background right after Minecraft draws it (NeoForge's container screen events) and redraws the labels in light colours. No textures are replaced, so resource packs for these screens are covered by it while the option is on.
