# Data Center AddOn 1.5.1

A bug-fix release for the switch LEDs introduced in 1.5.0.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**.

## Fixed
- **Switch LEDs sat next to the painted dots.** The switch fronts have 12 port dots in three groups of four. 1.5.0 lit only 8 of them, and only the first four were in the right place. All 12 now light exactly on the painted dots.
- **3Com SuperStack II hub:** its front has 8 dots spaced differently from the other switches, and they now get positions of their own.
