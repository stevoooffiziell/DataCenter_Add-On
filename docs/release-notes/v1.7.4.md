# Data Center AddOn 1.7.4

Part 5 of the new customer model: **every customer class opens a sub-pool of its own**. Climbing to the next class now brings new customers, not only a higher price.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Sub-pools per customer class
- The hobbyist pool is the market as before. Every class you have unlocked adds a sub-pool:

| Class | Reputation | Sub-pool | Reachable in total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hobbyists | 0 | – | ×1.00 |
| Small Business | 150 | +30 % | ×1.30 |
| Mid-Market | 350 | +30 % | ×1.60 |
| Enterprise | 600 | +25 % | ×1.85 |
| Finance & Government | 850 | +20 % | ×2.05 |

- Customers from the sub-pools lean to the better paying segments (company websites, shops, enterprise, game servers). The more of your potential comes from sub-pools, the better your new customers pay.
- The class counts as soon as it is unlocked by reputation. The income multiplier of a class still needs the room certification, as in Data Center.
- **Reputation itself** now raises the share from ×1 at 0 points to **×2** at 1000 points (was ×3). Together with the sub-pools a company at 1000 reputation reaches about 4 times what it reaches at 0 (was 3 times).

Example, shared web 2003 (market 3,250), fully known: 650 reachable at 0 reputation, 970 at 150 (small business), 1,430 at 400 (mid-market), 2,670 at 1000.

## Market tab
The tooltip of **Reachable** shows the factor of the customer classes.
