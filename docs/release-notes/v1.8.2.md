# Data Center AddOn 1.8.2

Part 3 of the new reputation model: **the Ruf**, a second value next to the reputation points. It says how good your service has been over the last 30 game days, from 0 to 100 %.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**, in exactly the same version.

## Ruf
- A sliding value of the actual quality of all your hosting racks (dedicated servers included) over the last **30 game days**:

  **Ruf = 100 % − 5 × downtime − ½ × overload − ½ × throttling − 2 % per crash**

  - **Downtime:** share of servers (or colocation customers) that did not run. 99 % uptime costs 5 %, 98 % costs 10 %.
  - **Overload:** how far serving servers ran over their real capacity.
  - **Throttling:** share of the serving that ran throttled by heat, an attack or a network too slow.
  - **Crashes** in the window.
- With the same quality the Ruf stays where it is. It does not grow by itself. Bad days leave the window after 30 days.
- Without any service yet the Ruf is 100 %.
- **Effect:** the Ruf scales the share of the markets you reach: ×0.5 at 0 %, ×1 at 100 % (×0.95 at 90 %).

## Reputation points
Throttling and downtime **no longer cost reputation points** by the hour. They lower the Ruf instead. Running servers neither earn nor lose points now. A crash still costs 20 points (that changes in a later release).

## Hosting Manager
- New tab **Reputation**: the Ruf with uptime, overload, throttling, crashes and the days in the window, and how it is computed.
- **Overview:** the reputation line shows the Ruf too.
- **Market tab:** the tooltip of *Reachable* shows the Ruf factor.
- With seven tabs the title gives way to the tabs in narrow windows.
