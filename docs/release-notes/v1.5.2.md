# Data Center AddOn 1.5.2

The Hosting Manager now counts the income of your contracts. Contracts keep places of your racks free, so what they pay is part of what your data center earns.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1 · Data Center 2.1.3 or newer
**Optional:** FTB Quests (for the guide and the calendar) · Jade 15 (for the look-at tooltips)
Install on the **server and every client**.

## Contract income in the Hosting Manager
- **Overview:** "Per day" and "Per month" now include what your running contracts pay per day. The table by product has an extra row **Contracts (n)** with the places they reserve, the share of contracts whose places are free right now, and their pay per day.
- **Customers tab:** every running contract has its own row, **Contract: client name**, highlighted in blue. It shows the reserved places, their share of all customers and the pay per day. The shares now include the contracts.
- **Jade:** the Hosting Manager's income per day includes the contracts as well.

A contract pays only for the days its places are kept free. The figures show the full daily pay, so a day that gets a strike earns less than shown.
